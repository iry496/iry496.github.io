Research is a way of thinking. I help you turn curiosity into a focused question, make sound methodological choices, and communicate what the evidence can—and cannot—tell us.

DEEPEN YOUR PRACTICE

University & graduate students

Strengthen study design, literature synthesis, statistical reasoning, reproducible analysis, and the explanation of methods and limitations.

Bring a new idea or a project already in progress.

How we work together

A clear process, adapted to your starting point.

  1. 01 /

    Define the question

    Clarify your interests, review what is known, and set a realistic scope.

  2. 02 /

    Build the evidence

    Choose methods, document your work, and test your assumptions.

  3. 03 /

    Explain your work

    Refine your analysis, discuss limitations, and communicate clearly.

You develop and explain your own work; I provide structured feedback and methodological guidance.

We agree on the scope, meeting format, and fees before beginning.
